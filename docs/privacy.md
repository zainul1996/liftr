# Privacy

LIFTR has no backend or analytics service. Data Collection Mode intentionally records wrist motion after you start it. Recordings contain pseudonymous participant and session IDs, exercise labels, optional weight and repetition counts, device details and timestamps. Do not use a real name as the participant ID.

Recordings remain in each device's app storage until you export or delete them. Exported files may be sensitive. Store them locally and keep them out of Git. iPhone deletion only removes that device's copy. Watch copies and external exports require separate deletion. HealthKit workout records, if saved, are managed through Apple's Health app.

No raw motion is collected without starting recording. No health data is sent to a server. Revisit retention controls, backup exclusion, platform data protection and App Store privacy declarations before distribution.
